The heart behind the name

Meet Mockie & Marie.

Every good story starts somewhere. Ours starts with two grandmothers: Grandma Cass and Mamaw Webb.

Mockie, our Grandma Cass, was a woman of quiet strength. Strong when she needed to be and gentle with the people she loved, she had a calm sense of humor and a warmth all her own.

Marie, our Mamaw Webb, was the mother of ten children. Outgoing, spirited, and never afraid to be stern, she loved her family fiercely — and nobody was leaving her house hungry.

Though they came from opposite sides of our family, these two women spent a great deal of time together. They shared laughter, conversation, and a bond that brought our families closer.

We created Mockie & Maries to carry a little of both women forward: Mockie's gentle warmth and Marie's generous hospitality. A place to share a story, catch up with a friend, and make memories over something good.

“From our family to yours, there's always room at the table.”
